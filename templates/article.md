# The useful question

Give the answer in a short paragraph. Say what it does and does not establish.

## Scope and prerequisites

Who is this for? What platform/configuration/version was checked? Which permissions or local files are needed? What remains unknown?

## The important distinction

Explain the concept that prevents a common error. Distinguish requests, referred visits, and citations when relevant. Link the primary source next to the claim.

## Reproduce the result

Provide tested steps or a working command and expected behavior. Mark synthetic inputs visibly. Link to the real tool and its Markdown instructions once they exist; do not invent endpoints.

## Interpret the result

Describe useful outcomes, failure cases, and what cannot be concluded. For any number, state the period, unit, denominator, method, and sample limits.

## Sources and review

Add primary source title, publisher, URL, actual checked date, and the claim supported. Separate a vendor's statement from an independently observed test and from our inference. Record real meaningful update dates, not the current deploy time.

## Next useful step

Link a closely related guide, reproducible experiment, or tool. Do not add a generic sales pitch or a boilerplate FAQ solely for keywords.
